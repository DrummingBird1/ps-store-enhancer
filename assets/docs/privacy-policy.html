Overview

PS Store Enhancer (formerly GameDeals+, then PS Store Insight) is a browser extension that enhances the PlayStation Store website. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the extension accesses, how it's used, and how it's stored.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No personal information — we do not collect your name, email, address, or payment information.
  • No analytics or tracking — we do not use Google Analytics, telemetry, or any tracking service.
  • No data sharing — we do not sell, share, or transmit your data to any third party.
  • No remote servers — all data is stored locally on your device. We do not operate any backend servers.

The extension adds no personal or identifying information to these requests; as with any web request, the server still sees your IP address and your browser's details (see also “Pictures” below). Only game titles are used as search queries. If you add games to your wishlist, their titles are sent to CheapShark once per day in the background to check for price drops; this can be disabled by removing items from your wishlist or turning the feature off in settings. As you type in the PlayStation Store's own search box, the text you've typed so far (2+ characters) is sent to CheapShark to show enriched suggestions (thumbnail, title, cheapest PC price) in a dropdown; this happens only while the search box has focus and can be turned off in settings ("Live Search Suggestions").

Pictures. Two kinds of pictures are loaded by your browser itself, from the server that hosts them, as on any web page: the small game thumbnails in the live search dropdown (the picture address comes from CheapShark's answer; it is usually on Steam's image server, steamstatic.com, and for some games on the image server of another shop such as GOG, Green Man Gaming, GamersGate, Humble Bundle or WinGameStore) and, if you connected PSN, your PSN avatar on the settings page (the address comes from Sony's profile service). Those servers can see what any image request shows them, such as your IP address, your browser version, which picture was asked for and, as the browser's referrer information, the page it is shown on (unless the browser withholds it). Nothing from the extension (no token, no account detail, no search text) is added to these requests. Because the browser makes them, they need no host permission and do not appear in the extension's network log. Turn off Live Search Suggestions to stop the thumbnails; the avatar goes when you disconnect PSN.

PSN Account Connection

Connecting your PSN account is entirely optional. If you choose to connect:

  • Your NPSSO authentication token is stored locally and used solely to communicate with Sony's API.
  • Your game library data (game titles and categories) is stored locally for the owned-games filter.
  • To sign in, the extension briefly places your token in the browser's cookie for Sony's sign-in domain and restores whatever cookie was there before (or removes it) as soon as the attempt finishes, so it never overwrites or leaves behind a Sony session.
  • You can disconnect your PSN account at any time from the extension's settings page, which immediately deletes all PSN-related data from local storage.
  • No PSN credentials or data are ever sent to any server other than Sony's official API endpoints.

Host access is limited to: store.playstation.com (UI injection, and the once-a-day read of wishlisted games' own pages), ca.account.sony.com & m.np.playstation.com (optional PSN), cheapshark.com (prices, Metacritic and Steam ratings), and api.frankfurter.dev (currency rates). The extension requests no other site access (showing a picture from another server, see Pictures above, needs none).

Reporting a problem

"Report a problem" (popup and settings page) copies the extension version, your browser string and the local diagnostics log to your clipboard and opens the project's bug-report form on GitHub in a new tab. The log never contains tokens, codes or query strings. Nothing is sent by the extension itself: you decide whether to paste the text into the form.

Fonts

The font used by the extension (Rubik, SIL Open Font License) is packaged with it and is never downloaded: the extension's pages and the cards on store pages read the font files from the extension's own package. No font, script or stylesheet is loaded from a server. The only things your browser loads from other servers are the pictures described under Pictures above.

Sharing a wishlist

"Copy share link" (settings page, your action only) builds a link to a static page on this website with your wishlist encoded in the part of the URL after the # — game names and last-seen PC prices only, no targets or notes. Browsers never send that part of a URL to a server, so nothing is uploaded or stored; anyone you give the link to can read the list.

Data Retention

  • All data is stored locally on your device using Chrome's storage API.
  • Cached API responses expire automatically (4-24 hours depending on type).
  • Uninstalling the extension removes all stored data.
  • You can manually clear all cached data from the extension's settings page.
  • Erase all my data (settings page and popup) disconnects PSN and deletes everything the extension stored locally — wishlist, owned games, caches, recorded prices and the diagnostics and network logs. Your preference settings are kept.

Children's Privacy

This extension does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The extension does not collect personal information from any user.
