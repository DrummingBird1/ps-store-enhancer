PS Store Enhancer (formerly GameDeals+) adds real price insight, review scores and smart filters to the PlayStation™ Store, so you can decide what to buy based on facts. Every core feature works out of the box — no sign-in, no account.

🔍 PRICE INTELLIGENCE
• Real lowest-ever PC price (with its date), today's cheapest price and a “near the all-time low” flag — real data only, no estimated charts
• A price trend drawn from the prices your own browser recorded
• Cross-platform comparison for the exact game (never a DLC, map or sequel): Steam, Epic Games Store, GOG, Humble Store and more — powered by CheapShark and converted to your currency
• Edition transparency: a price from a different edition or bundle (Deluxe, Ultimate, GOTY…) is clearly flagged, never blended in silently
• Hints about when the big sales usually start
• Live search suggestions: as you type in the store's search box, see box art and the cheapest PC price
• One-click link to PSPrices for the full PlayStation price history

📊 REVIEW SCORES
• Metacritic (PC) and Steam user-rating badges next to the game title (via CheapShark)
• Color-coded green, yellow or red — shown only when a real score exists
• Click a badge to open the full reviews

🏆 COMPLETIONIST INFO
• Platinum availability, difficulty (1–10), estimated time and playthroughs for 75 popular games — a community-editable database on GitHub
• A one-glance completionist effort score and price per hour
• “Similar difficulty” picks from your own library
• Optional, with PSN connected: your live trophy progress and, experimentally, your rarest remaining trophy

🧹 SMART SEARCH FILTERS
• Hide add-ons, currency packs and starter packs
• Hide DLC, expansions and season passes
• Hide games you already own (your manual list + optional PSN sync)
• “Mark as owned” button on every product page
• ✓ / ★ marks in search suggestions for games you own or have wishlisted
• Works with infinite scroll; press “/” to jump to the store's search box

⭐ WISHLIST WITH PRICE ALERTS
• Add a game with one click on its product page
• Set a price or “% off” target, add notes, sort and filter
• Get a desktop notification — or one weekly digest — when the cheapest PC price reaches your target
• Records the PS Store price you see on each game's page
• Share your list as a read-only link: the list lives inside the link, nothing is uploaded
• Daily background checks, no constant polling
• Toolbar badge counts the wishlist deals at or below your target
• Library & Wishlist dashboard: games owned, wishlist size, total value, deals ready to buy — all computed from data on your device

💱 CURRENCY CONVERSION
• Cross-platform prices convert from USD to your local currency automatically
• 18 currencies (EUR, GBP, ILS, JPY, BRL, KRW, MXN, INR and more), set from your store region
• Daily exchange rates from Frankfurter (European Central Bank data)

🎨 THEMES & EXTRAS
• Light, Dark, High-contrast and Auto themes
• Collapse, reorder or hide the product-page cards; share a deal as an image
• Right-click any text on the PS Store and choose “Find on PS Store Enhancer price compare” for an instant CheapShark lookup
• Detects your PS Store region from the URL on first visit
• Back up and restore your owned-games list and wishlist as JSON

♿ ACCESSIBILITY
• Respects your reduced-motion setting
• Screen-reader announcements when cards load
• Visible keyboard focus everywhere, plus the high-contrast theme

🔗 OPTIONAL: PSN LIBRARY SYNC (advanced)
• Off by default — it needs your own PSN account, and everything else works without it
• Click Connect and paste your NPSSO token (step-by-step guide inside the extension), or let the extension detect it if you are signed in to PlayStation in this browser
• Games from your PSN played-games list count as owned: they are hidden from search results and re-synced every 2 hours
• Chrome may ask for one optional permission, only when you click Connect — it is used solely to read Sony's sign-in redirect
• Your own Sony cookie is restored afterwards and the token stays on your device
• Disconnect anytime; your manual entries are kept

⚡ PERFORMANCE
• Smart caching keeps API calls to a minimum
• Skeleton loading animations while data loads
• Lightweight: no external libraries, plain JavaScript

🌍 16 LANGUAGES
English, עברית, العربية, Español, Français, Deutsch, Italiano, Português (BR), Nederlands, Polski, Türkçe, Русский, 日本語, 한국어, 简体中文, 繁體中文
Built-in language selector — override your browser language in Settings.

🔒 PRIVACY FIRST
• All your data stays on your device
• No analytics, no tracking, no data sharing
• No servers of our own: the only requests go to CheapShark (prices, scores), Frankfurter (exchange rates) and — only if you connect — Sony
• “Erase all my data” button in the popup and in Settings
• A local diagnostics log that never records tokens or passwords
• Open source (MIT) on GitHub; full privacy policy available
